- Extract keywords ONLY from the job description

  - Methodologies

1. Extract a list of important ATS keywords from the job description
2. Compare each keyword against the resume

   - present_keywords → explicitly mentioned or clearly implied
   - missing_keywords → not present or weakly represented

   (len(present_keywords) / len(jd_keywords)) * 100

- present_keywords MUST be a subset of jd_keywords
- missing_keywords MUST be jd_keywords minus present_keywords
